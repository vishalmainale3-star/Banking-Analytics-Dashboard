# Banking Analytics Dashboard | Excel & Power BI
##  Project Objectives
Analyze banking loan performance and disbursement trends.
Monitor loan collections and interest revenue.
Identify loan defaults and delinquency patterns.
Evaluate branch-wise and state-wise loan distribution.
Analyze customer debit and credit transactions.
Understand transaction behaviour across banks and branches.
Develop interactive dashboards for data-driven decision-making.

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## 1. Bank Loan Dashboard

1. **Total Loan Amount Funded** – Measures the total amount of loans disbursed by the bank.
2. **Total Loans** – Represents the total number of loans issued.
3. **Total Collection** – Measures the total amount collected through loan repayments.
4. **Total Interest** – Represents the total interest revenue generated from loans.
5. **Branch-Wise Performance** – Analyses branch-wise interest, fees, and total revenue.
6. **State-Wise Loan Distribution** – Evaluates loan distribution across different states.
7. **Religion-Wise Loan Distribution** – Analyses loan distribution across different religious demographics.
8. **Product Group-Wise Loan** – Examines loan distribution across different product categories.
9. **Disbursement Trend** – Tracks loan disbursement patterns over time.
10. **Grade-Wise Loan Analysis** – Evaluates loan distribution based on borrower grades.
11. **Default Loan Count** – Measures the total number of loans that have defaulted.
12. **Delinquent Client Count** – Identifies the number of borrowers with missed payments.
13. **Delinquent Loan Rate** – Measures the percentage of delinquent loans in the portfolio.
14. **Default Loan Rate** – Measures the percentage of defaulted loans in the portfolio.
15. **Loan Status-Wise Analysis** – Analyses loans based on active, closed, delinquent, and defaulted statuses.
16. **Age Group-Wise Loan Distribution** – Examines loan distribution across different borrower age groups.
17. **Loan Maturity** – Tracks the duration until loans reach their repayment maturity.
18. **No Verified Loan** – Identifies loans that have not been verified.

## 2. Bank Debit and Credit Dashboard

19. **Total Credit Amount** – Measures the total amount credited through banking transactions.
20. **Total Debit Amount** – Measures the total amount debited through banking transactions.
21. **Credit-to-Debit Ratio** – Compares total credit amounts with total debit amounts.
22. **Net Transaction Amount** – Measures the difference between total credit and debit amounts.
23. **Account Activity Ratio** – Evaluates account activity based on transaction behaviour.
24. **Transactions per Day/Week/Month** – Tracks transaction counts across different time periods.
25. **Total Transaction Amount by Branch** – Compares transaction amounts across different branches.
26. **Transaction Volume by Bank** – Evaluates transaction volumes across different banks.
27. **Transaction Method Distribution** – Analyses transactions based on different payment methods.
28. **Branch Transaction Growth** – Measures the percentage growth or decline in branch transaction volumes.
29. **High-Risk Transaction Flag** – Identifies transactions flagged as potentially high-risk.
30. **Suspicious Transaction Frequency** – Tracks the frequency of flagged transactions over a specific period.

## 1. Bank Loan Dashboard – Project Process

1. **Data Preparation:** Imported the banking loan dataset into Excel and reviewed loan, borrower, repayment, and branch-related information.

2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies to prepare the dataset for analysis.

3. **KPI Calculation:** Calculated key metrics such as Total Loan Amount Funded, Total Loans, Total Collection, Total Interest, Default Loan Rate, and Delinquent Loan Rate.

4. **Data Analysis:** Analysed branch-wise and state-wise loan distribution, product group performance, borrower age groups, loan grades, and disbursement trends.

5. **Dashboard Development:** Created interactive dashboards using Excel, Tableau, and Power BI to visualize loan performance and portfolio risk.

6. **Validation and Insights:** Compared dashboard results with database records, evaluated loan repayment performance, and identified default and delinquency patterns.

---

## 2. Bank Debit and Credit Dashboard – Project Process

1. **Data Preparation:** Imported the debit and credit banking dataset containing customer details, transaction dates, transaction types, amounts, balances, branches, and bank names.

2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent transaction records to improve data quality.

3. **KPI Calculation:** Calculated Total Credit Amount, Total Debit Amount, Credit-to-Debit Ratio, Net Transaction Amount, and Account Activity Ratio.

4. **Transaction Analysis:** Analysed daily, weekly, and monthly transactions, branch-wise transaction amounts, bank-wise transaction volume, and transaction methods.

5. **Dashboard Development:** Created interactive dashboards using Excel, Tableau, and Power BI to visualize debit and credit activities and transaction patterns.

6. **Validation and Insights:** Validated dashboard results against database records and analysed customer activity, high-value transactions, and unusual transaction patterns.

## Project Insights

### 1. Bank Loan Dashboard – Key Insights

1. **Loan Performance:** Analysed total loan amounts funded, total loans issued, collections, and interest revenue to understand overall lending performance.

2. **Branch and State Analysis:** Evaluated branch-wise revenue and state-wise loan distribution to understand regional lending performance.

3. **Product Analysis:** Identified loan distribution across different product groups to understand lending patterns and product performance.

4. **Loan Repayment Analysis:** Monitored total collections, delinquent clients, and loan repayment status to evaluate repayment performance.

5. **Credit Risk Analysis:** Analysed default loans, delinquent loan rates, and borrower credit grades to understand portfolio risk.

6. **Customer Demographics:** Examined age-group-wise and religion-wise loan distribution to understand borrower demographics and lending patterns.

### 2. Bank Debit and Credit Dashboard – Key Insights

1. **Transaction Analysis:** Analysed total credit amounts and total debit amounts to understand overall transaction activities.

2. **Cash Flow Analysis:** Used the credit-to-debit ratio and net transaction amount to evaluate the relationship between deposits and withdrawals.

3. **Account Activity:** Examined account activity ratios to understand customer transaction behaviour.

4. **Time-Based Analysis:** Analysed daily, weekly, and monthly transaction volumes to identify transaction activity trends.

5. **Branch and Bank Performance:** Compared transaction amounts across branches and banks to understand transaction distribution.

6. **Transaction Monitoring:** Examined transaction methods, high-risk transaction flags, and suspicious transaction frequency to identify unusual transaction patterns.
